HOW TO PLAY
  1. Every club has a stock yardage. Pulling back 100% hits that full distance.
  2. Check PIN at the top for yards remaining, pick a club, then pull back just enough. For example, 120 yards with a 150-yard club is about 80%.
  3. Swipe left or right to aim. Tap the club name to open your bag, or use ◀ ▶.
  4. Press the ball and pull back. The needle swings across the gauge. Let go in the bright gold zone for a perfect strike.
  5. Rough cuts distance 15% and sand cuts it 40% (less with the Sand Wedge). Watch the wind arrow.
  6. Water and out of bounds cost a stroke. MAP shows the whole hole, and you can tap it to aim.
  7. Win money for par or better. Upgrade clubs in the Clubs tab for more yards, a slower needle and a bigger gold zone.
